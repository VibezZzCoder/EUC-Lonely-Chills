## A companion to EUC Thrills

This game is a companion to *EUC Thrills*, an earlier electric-unicycle
browser game by the same author: <https://github.com/VibezZzCoder/EUC-thrills>. Four of its
riders, Cool Rider, Trollina, The Drunkard and Officer Dorkins, appear in this
game, and some of this game's code and sounds were first made for it by the
same author. *EUC Thrills* is a separate work with its own licence,
found in its repository. That licence does not cover this game, and this
game's licence does not cover *EUC Thrills*.

The author published *EUC Thrills*'s source code under the MIT License
and its original assets under CC BY 4.0. Material this game shares with it
keeps those terms as published there: the code this game adapted from it,
the models and looks of the four riders, and four of the sound files listed
under *Sounds* (Cool Rider's and Officer Dorkins' crashes, the wheel on solid
ground and the over-speed alarm). This game's licence covers this game's own
versions; it takes away none of the rights those licences give you in that
material as published in *EUC Thrills*.

## Made in code

The game's world is built by its own code as it runs: the island's terrain,
the trails and everything along them, the trees, plants and rocks, the
riders, their wheels and their painted textures, and the sky and its light.
The game contains no image, model or font files, and the text uses your
device's own fonts.

Most of what you hear is synthesized by that code while you play. The
exceptions are nine short sound files, listed under *Sounds*, which are built
into the game's page. The only images in this package are the icons, drawn
by the project's own code, and the screenshots in `media/`, captured from
the game.

## Sounds

Everything audible that is not in this list, including the nature sounds
(birds, wind in the trees, creeks and the lake shore), thumps and chimes, is
synthesized at runtime and contains no recording.

CC0 requires no attribution; they are credited here with thanks. Both were

**Four original files by the author.**

| In the game | What it is |
|---|---|
| Cool Rider's crash | The author's own recording of the author's own fall, voice included. |
| Officer Dorkins' crash | The same recording with the author's voice removed. |
| The wheel on solid ground | A tap loop synthesized from scratch by the author's own tool. |
| The over-speed alarm | A tone rebuilt from measurements of a real alarm. It contains no audio from the reference that was measured. |

**Three composed files with a machine-generated vocal layer.**

| In the game | What it is |
|---|---|
| Trollina's crash | Slapstick sound synthesized by the author's scripts, under vocal beats. |
| The Drunkard's crash | Slapstick sound synthesized by the author's scripts, under one cheerful, slurred vocal line. |
| The Drunkard's stumble | Two synthesized can clanks under one vocal "hic". |

The vocal beats in these three files were produced with a text-to-speech
model (OpenAI `gpt-audio`) and then cut, re-timed, pitch-shifted and mixed. No
copyright is claimed over that machine-generated vocal layer. No real
person's voice is in any of them.

## Places, people and brands

Hushwater Isle, its six trails and its landmarks are fictional.
The riders, Cool Rider, Trollina, The Drunkard and Officer Dorkins, are the
author's own characters. No rider or wheel carries a real logo or any
lettering: The Drunkard's hop-cone motif and Officer Dorkins' shield-shaped
badge are original shapes drawn in code, not any real brewery's mark or any
real force's crest. The Drunkard is a wholly fictional parody. Officer
Dorkins is a fictional, comic officer: the game is not affiliated with,
endorsed by or sponsored by any police force or government agency.

The electric unicycles in the game are generic, fictional designs drawn in
code, not models of any real product. The game is not affiliated with,
endorsed by or sponsored by any electric-unicycle maker, brand, business or
organization. Any resemblance of a wheel or place to a real one is coincidental.
